How the maths works

7-day avg weight = mean of weigh-ins in the last 7 days.
7-day weight delta = this week's avg minus last week's avg (needs 4+ weigh-ins in each).
Daily deficit = −delta × energy per kg ÷ 7.
Rolling TDEE = avg intake over the 14 days ending yesterday + daily deficit (needs 10+ logged days).
Budget = latest rolling TDEE − target deficit. Before that exists it uses your starting estimate, then SmartThings' 7-day average as a last resort.
SmartThings accuracy = SmartThings 14-day avg burn vs rolling TDEE over the same days.
